Freshpet PM — the whole job on one card

Keep this in the van. Everything on this side is what makes a report we can stand behind.

Three photos on every single unit. No exceptions.

1
Full pic of unit
The whole cabinet, where it stands, in the store.
2
Serial plate
Close enough to read the numbers. Of this machine.
3
Bottom of unit
Underneath — condenser and floor.
Take them with the camera buttons in the app, at the unit, while you are standing there. The app will not let you submit until all three are on the report, and it will tell you which one is missing.

Every stop, in order

  1. Open My Route. Each store is a card. Every cooler at that store is listed on it with its model and serial — match the card to the machine in front of you before you touch anything.
  2. Tap the unit. That opens its PM. Check the serial on screen against the plate on the machine.
  3. Do the work, then fill the form from what you see. Nothing is pre-filled any more. The temperature, the set point and the voltage are blank because they are readings, not defaults.
  4. Take the three photos.
  5. Get the store signature — or tick No store contact available to sign. Never sign the store's box yourself.
  6. Submit. You land back on the store card. The next cooler is right there — you do not go back out to the main screen. A cooler you have finished drops off the card, so what is left on screen is what you still owe; the whole store disappears from the route once every cooler on it is done.

The comments box

  • Anything you write there goes to the office as a reported issue — damage, a unit needing service, anything wrong. Somebody reads it and answers it, so leave it blank on a normal visit.
  • A note is not a record. If what you are reporting is a cooler that is not on the list, use + Add an asset too — that is what puts it on the books.

Save your own copies

  • Tap Save to my phone after you submit. A panel comes up on the next screen offering the photos from the stop you just finished. Tap it, then choose Save Image (or Save to Photos). That is the only way they reach your camera roll.
  • They do NOT save themselves. A photo you take with the app's camera button is taken inside the app — it never lands in your camera roll on its own. If you skip that panel there is no copy on your phone. (A picture you took with the normal Camera app first and then picked is already in your roll.)
  • Keep about a month — that covers the gap between doing the work and anyone asking about the invoice. Your copy settles it.
  • No camera setting to change. The app shrinks each photo itself (a few MB → about 100 KB) so a route uploads on bad store signal — but the copy it hands back to your phone is the full-size original.
  • If a photo will not upload, do not delete it — finish the stop, then tell the office which store and unit.
Why all of this changed. A customer reviewed one invoice and found the same photograph on several different reports, forms completed for stores that were closed, and units with no picture of their own serial plate. It cost us the invoice and a lot of trust. Every rule on this card exists to stop one of those things. None of it is about you working slower — it is about the report being able to prove what you did.
Questions, or anything on this card does not match what the app is doing: service@brixbev.com.

What if…

The short answer to everything that goes wrong at a stop.
The store is closed. Locked, papered over, gone. On the store card, bottom strip: Store closed.
Red form. Photograph the storefront, write what you saw. It covers every unit at that store in one go — you do not file one per cooler.
The store is open but one machine is not there. Open that unit, then on its asset card at the top: MISSING ASSET — this unit is not on site.
Red form. Photograph where it should be, say who you asked.
There is a cooler here that is not on my list. Store card → + Add an asset. Enter what you can read off it, then do the full PM on it. It gets flagged for the office as a new asset, and Freshpet sees it on their own portal until they add it to their list. Writing it in Comments instead is not enough — a note tells the office, only Add an asset puts the unit on the books.
I cannot find or read the serial plate. On the PM form, above the photo buttons, tick NO SERIAL NUMBER — plate missing or unreadable.
The plate photo is replaced by Inside of cooler — shelves and interior. Still three photos. The report says NO SERIAL NUMBER FOUND and goes to the office by itself.
If the unit is not on your list and has no plate: add it with the same NO SERIAL NUMBER box ticked.
Something is wrong with the unit — broken, leaking, iced up, unsafe. Finish the form as far as you can, write it in the comments, then REPORT ISSUE at the bottom. The report is flagged and emailed to the office.
The form is blue. You are on a SECONDARY VISIT AUDIT — a stop being re-documented because the first report could not be stood behind. Do the PM properly and take fresh photos; nothing carries over from last time. You are paid your normal rate for it.
Nobody is around to sign. Tick No store contact available to sign. The report states it plainly. Never sign for the store.
I have no meter for the voltage. Tick No meter on hand. The report prints not measured (no meter). A stated gap is fine; a made-up number is not.
I already did this unit and something is wrong on the report. Finished work is hidden, not gone. On a part-done store tap Show N already done at this store; for a store you have closed out, tap Already done at the bottom of the route. Then tap the unit — it reopens for editing. The change is timestamped; the original stays on file.
The app says my photo was already used on another report. That is the duplicate guard, and it is right. Take a new photo of this machine, now. Never pull one out of your gallery.
I lost signal in the store. Finish the form. It is held on the phone and picks up where you left off. Get outside or to the van and submit — do not start it over.
Someone else already serviced this unit. PM PREV COMP at the bottom of the form. It records that it was done elsewhere and is not billed.
The one rule behind all of them: the report should be able to prove what you did without you being in the room. A photo of the machine you actually touched, a reading you actually took, and a plain sentence when something was not possible. "I could not do it and here is why" is always a better report than a form that looks complete.
