Three photos on every unit, taken with the app's camera buttons while you stand there. The app won't let you submit without all three, and it names the one that's missing.

The stop

  1. My Route → each store is a card, every cooler listed on it with model + serial. Match the card to the machine first.
  2. Tap the unit to open its PM. Check the serial on screen against the plate.
  3. Do the work, then fill the form. Nothing is pre-filled — temp, set point and voltage are blank because they're readings.
  4. Three photos.
  5. Store signature, or tick No store contact available. Never sign their box.
  6. Submit → you land back on the store card, next cooler right there.

Your phone

  • After you submit, tap Save to my phone → Save Image. Photos taken with the app's camera button do not reach your camera roll on their own — that panel is the only way.
  • Keep them ~1 month. If a report is ever questioned, your copy settles it. Don't clear the camera roll after a route.
  • No camera setting to change. The app shrinks each photo itself (a few MB → about 100 KB) so a route uploads on bad store signal — but the copy it saves to your phone is the full-size original.
  • Photo won't upload? Don't delete it. Finish the stop, then tell the office the store and unit.
  • Lost signal? Finish the form — it's held on the phone. Submit outside. Don't start over.

The one rule behind all of it: the report has to prove what you did without you being in the room — a photo of the machine you actually touched, a reading you actually took, and a plain sentence when something wasn't possible. "I couldn't do it and here's why" always beats a form that looks complete.
